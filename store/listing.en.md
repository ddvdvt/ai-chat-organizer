## Detailed description

Hundreds of AI chats in one endless list? This extension files them into folders, finds the right one by its text and saves it to a file. Free, no sign-up, no servers.

Features

• Folders and subfolders, pins, coloured tags. The same folders for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.
• A panel right on the conversation page: file the open conversation into a folder, pin it, tag it, export it.
• Drag a conversation from the site's sidebar onto a folder — or use the small button that appears on hover.
• An organizer tab: all chats of the three services, filters by folder, tag and service, bulk moves.
• Search the text of conversations you have opened: words, "exact phrases", -exclusions, highlighted matches.
• Export to Markdown, HTML, JSON, TXT and PDF. Code keeps its language, tables stay tables, formulas become TeX.
• Many conversations at once as a single .zip.
• A prompt library with variables: "Explain {{topic}} simply" — the extension asks for the value and inserts the text into the input box.
• Backup of folders and prompts to a file.
• Light and dark themes, Russian and English UI.

How to use

1. Click the toolbar icon and enable the sites where you chat with AI. The browser asks for permission for each site separately.
2. Open a conversation — the organizer button appears on the right edge.
3. Create a folder and drag conversations from the list on the left into it.

Privacy

Conversation text is read only from the page you opened yourself and stays in your browser. The extension never calls the services' APIs and never downloads your history. Saving for search can be turned off per site. No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no network requests.

Limitations

Services change their markup from time to time. Then some features switch off temporarily and the extension says "site changed" until an update ships. Attachments and images are not saved. Folders live in this browser and do not sync across devices.
